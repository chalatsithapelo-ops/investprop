Thank you for considering Investprop as a partner for your property at [Property Address]. Many owners are sitting on valuable property or land that they cannot unlock — developing, renovating or flipping takes capital, a professional team, approvals, funding relationships and years of risk. Most owners either sell cheaply and watch someone else make the profit, or let the asset sit idle while rates and holding costs mount.

We offer a third way. You contribute your property or land into a dedicated company (an SPV), and in exchange you receive a 20% shareholding in that company. Investprop funds and manages the entire project — feasibility, approvals, design, funding, construction or renovation and sales — at our risk and cost. When the project is sold or let, you share in the proceeds as a 20% shareholder, having put in no cash and taken on none of the execution risk.

In one line: You keep ownership upside without selling cheap, without spending a cent, and without lifting a finger — we do everything.

Why Owners Choose This

  • You don’t sell cheap. Instead of a once-off sale at current value, you keep a stake in the far higher completed value.
  • You risk no money. Every rand of project cost and funding is ours. You never receive a capital call.
  • You carry none of the execution burden. We manage the professionals, the municipality, the bank and the builders.
  • Your asset is ring-fenced. Your property sits in its own company, legally separated from any other project’s liabilities.
  • Full transparency. You see the cap table, capital movements and distributions through the platform.
