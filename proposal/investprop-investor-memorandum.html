1. Executive Summary

Property is South Africa’s most trusted store of wealth, and institutional funders — banks, development-finance institutions (DFIs), and large investors — are willing to finance strong property-development projects. In practice, however, they will only do so if the developer contributes a meaningful equity stake, typically a minimum of around 30% of project cost. That co-contribution is the single biggest reason good projects stall: most capable developers simply do not have 30% of a project’s cost sitting in cash.

Investprop exists to close that gap. We are a live, AI-powered platform that aggregates the required developer equity from a pool of qualifying co-investors — each contributing from [R10,000] — and pairs that pooled equity with institutional development finance. The pooled equity and the project are held inside ring-fenced Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs), with transparent reporting, share certificates, AI-driven underwriting, and a path to secondary liquidity.

This creates a two-sided invitation. For everyday and qualifying investors, it is a chance to co-invest in institutional-grade developments that were previously out of reach. For institutional funders and development-finance partners, Investprop is the counterparty that reliably assembles the ~30% equity tranche, originates and underwrites deals with AI, and administers them in a compliant, auditable structure — de-risking and unlocking deal flow they could not otherwise transact.

The platform is live today as a production web application and a native mobile app (Android/iOS), with the co-investment pooling, SPV accounting, investor wallets, instant settlement, and share-integrity controls already built. Investprop earns diversified, recurring and transaction-based fees across the capital it helps raise, the distributions it pays out, and the secondary trades it facilitates.

2. Why Investprop Exists — The 30% Developer-Equity Problem

This platform was born out of a real, recurring obstacle we faced ourselves. Institutional funders — the PIC, banks such as Absa, Nedbank and Capitec, and specialist development financiers such as TUHF Capital — are genuinely willing to fund property developments. But almost all of them attach the same condition:

“Show us that you, the developer, have skin in the game — contribute a minimum of roughly 30% of the project equity, and we will fund the rest.”

For a [R50 million] development, that 30% is [R15 million] in cash — before a single brick is laid. Most competent developers, including us, do not have that amount idle. The result is a stalemate that holds back otherwise bankable, job-creating, housing-delivering projects:

  • The funder is ready but cannot deploy without the equity cushion.
  • The developer is capable but is blocked purely by the equity gap.
  • Everyday investors want property exposure but cannot access institutional-scale developments.

Investprop turns that stalemate into a transaction: the 30% is assembled from many qualifying co-investors, each taking a small, transparent, ring-fenced slice — and the institutional funder provides the senior finance on top.

The other barriers we also remove

  • Capital barrier: fractional tickets from [R10,000] replace the need for a whole-property deposit.
  • Opacity & trust: informal syndications are replaced with ring-fenced SPVs, share certificates, and transparent reporting.
  • Illiquidity: a built-in secondary market lets co-investors sell shares before project exit.
  • Compliance: FICA/KYC, POPIA-aware handling, and audit logging are built into the Platform.

3. The Solution — How the Pool Unlocks the Deal

Investprop sits between the institutional funder and the crowd, assembling a complete, fundable capital stack for each project. A typical structure looks like this:

LayerWho provides itTypical shareRole
Senior development financeBank / DFI / institutional funder (e.g. PIC, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec, TUHF Capital)[~70%]Primary project funding, drawn against the equity cushion below.
Pooled developer equityInvestprop co-investors (many small tickets)[~30%]The equity contribution the funder requires — aggregated by the Platform.
Origination, underwriting & adminInvestprop Platform—Sources the deal, grades it with AI, structures the SPV, and administers reporting, distributions and compliance.

The pooled equity is held in a ring-fenced SPV created for that single project. Each co-investor owns shares in the SPV proportional to their contribution, receives a share certificate, and sees transparent reporting on the underlying property. The institutional funder lends or co-invests against a project that now has its required equity cushion in place — a structure it recognises and can transact.

The unlock: neither the crowd alone nor the funder alone can do these deals. Pooled retail/qualifying equity + institutional finance + AI-driven origination and compliant administration together make a previously impossible project bankable.

8. How Capital Is Structured & Protected

Because the Platform aggregates other people’s money to form the developer-equity tranche, the integrity of how that capital is held, settled, and reconciled is central. These controls are already built and live:

ControlHow it protects capital
Ring-fenced SPVsEach project sits in its own Special Purpose Vehicle, so one deal’s performance and liabilities never contaminate another. Co-investors own shares in that single SPV.
In-app investor walletsCo-investors fund a wallet (via Paystack EFT/card) that is automatically reconciled — removing manual proof-of-payment and the errors that come with it.
Instant Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP)On the secondary market, shares and cash change hands atomically from the wallet: the buyer is debited and the seller credited in the same settled transaction, so there is never delivery without payment.
Share-integrity & conservation auditThe system continuously proves that no duplicate or phantom shares can exist — issued shares reconcile to the ledger per class — and traces every holding back to its property and SPV.
Provenance & certificatesEvery co-investor can see exactly how their shares were acquired, their valuation, and the property/SPV they map to, backed by a share certificate.
Auditable money trailDeposits, trades, distributions, fees, and withdrawals are each booked as signed, reference-tagged ledger entries for clean reporting and audit.

For an institutional funder, these are exactly the assurances needed to rely on the equity tranche: the ~30% is real, ring-fenced, reconciled, and administered — not an informal collection of IOUs.

14. Regulatory, Compliance & Risk

Fractional property co-investment touches South African financial-services regulation. Investprop’s strategy is to operate the regulated co-investment product through an FSCA-accredited partner that provides the licence and product-structuring oversight, while Investprop provides the technology, origination, and operations. The tool/SaaS and seller-facilitation lines are lighter-touch and can scale in parallel.
