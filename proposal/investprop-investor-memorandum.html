1. Executive Summary

At the same time, experienced investors lack a single, data-driven tool to source below-market deals, underwrite them objectively, and manage a portfolio end-to-end.

Investprop is a fully-built, AI-powered platform that solves both sides of this market. It makes property investment accessible from [R10,000] through fractional ownership held inside ring-fenced Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs), and it arms serious investors with an AI underwriting co-pilot, a nationwide distressed-property finder, institutional-grade deal analytics, and a full seller/landlord toolkit.

The platform is live today as a production web application and a native mobile app (Android/iOS), built on a modern, secure, type-safe stack. Revenue is diversified across recurring SaaS subscriptions and transaction-based platform fees on capital raised, distributions, secondary trades, and seller-side facilitation — so the business earns both while deals are sourced and while they are held.

2. The Problem

For everyday investors

  • Capital barrier: A single buy-to-let property requires a large deposit, bond qualification, and transfer costs — excluding most South Africans.
  • Opacity & trust: Private property syndications are often informal, with weak reporting and governance.
  • Illiquidity: Exiting a direct property holding is slow and expensive.
  • Compliance: FICA, POPIA, and financial-services rules are hard for informal operators to meet.

For serious / professional investors

  • Deal sourcing is manual: Distressed and below-market stock is scattered across auction houses, bank repossession pages, and sheriff sales.
  • Underwriting is inconsistent: Investors rely on spreadsheets and gut feel, with no independent grade on a deal.
  • No single cockpit: Sourcing, analysis, co-investment, and portfolio reporting live in different places.

For sellers & landlords

  • Selling to a cash/JV buyer is slow and opaque; fees are hidden; there is no fast, structured route to a motivated institutional buyer.

4. Product & Technology

The platform is production-grade and already shipping across two clients:

  • Web application — full investor, owner, and operations experience.
  • Native mobile app — Flutter-based Android/iOS app with the investor, seller, distressed-deals, and deal-calculator experiences.
  • Operations console — staff workflows for deal pipeline, compliance (FICA/KYC), revenue, and reporting.

Engineering highlights

  • Modern type-safe stack (TypeScript, React, tRPC, Prisma, PostgreSQL) with a hardened, containerised production deployment.
  • Security-first: bank-grade password hashing, role-based access control, rate limiting, input sanitisation, SSRF guards on the scraper, and an audit log on sensitive actions.
  • Automated schedulers for distressed-deal scraping, AI grading, alerting, and nightly market analysis.
  • Payments integration (Paystack) for subscriptions and recurring billing.

8. Unit Economics (Illustrative)

The model earns on both the subscription and the deal. A single Pro subscriber at R799/month contributes ~R9,588/year of high-margin recurring revenue. Separately, a R1,000,000 co-investment deal generates ~R25,000 at capital-raise (2.5%), plus distribution fees over the hold, plus any secondary-trade fees on exit — all on top of subscription revenue.

Key point: because sourcing, subscriptions, transactions, and seller-side fees stack on the same user base, lifetime value compounds as a user moves from browsing to subscribing to transacting.

12. Regulatory, Compliance & Risk

Fractional property co-investment touches South African financial-services regulation. Investprop’s strategy is to operate the regulated co-investment product through an FSCA-accredited partner that provides the licence and product-structuring oversight, while Investprop provides the technology, origination, and operations. The tool/SaaS and seller-facilitation lines are lighter-touch and can scale in parallel.

  • FICA / AML: identity verification and KYC workflows built in.
  • POPIA: privacy-aware data handling and consent.
  • Governance: ring-fenced SPVs, audit logging, and transparent fee disclosure.
