1. Executive Summary

Property is South Africa’s most trusted store of wealth, yet direct participation remains out of reach for most people because of high capital barriers, opacity, illiquidity, and heavy compliance requirements. Investprop is a fully-built, AI-powered platform that makes property investment accessible from [R10,000] through fractional ownership held inside ring-fenced Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs), with transparent reporting, tradable share certificates, and bank-grade FICA and POPIA processes.

To bring this offering to market in full compliance with South African financial-services law, Investprop is seeking a partnership with a licensed, FSCA-accredited institution. Investprop provides the technology, deal origination, operations, and customer experience; the Partner provides the regulated licence, oversight, and approved product structuring. Together we can deliver a compliant, scalable, and differentiated product to a large, underserved market.

4. Why a Regulated Partner — The Regulatory Rationale

Investprop is not currently FSCA-accredited. Offering fractional, pooled, or managed investment products to the public in South Africa engages a number of regulated activities. Rather than delay market entry, we have chosen the responsible route: partnering with an established, licensed institution that already holds the relevant accreditation and can provide proper regulatory oversight.

Regulatory areas the partnership is intended to address

AreaRelevance to the offering

5. Proposed Partnership Models

We propose exploring one (or a combination) of the following structures, to be finalised with the Partner:

Model A — Licensed CIS / Product Manager

The Partner acts as the licensed manager of the investment product (e.g. a collective investment or approved structured product). Investprop provides technology, origination, administration, and distribution support under the Partner’s licence and oversight.

Model B — FSP Mandate & Juristic Representative

The Partner (a licensed Category I/II FSP) appoints Investprop and/or its key individuals as representatives under a mandate, with the Partner supervising the regulated activities and approving all client-facing material and processes.

Model C — White-Label / Approved Arrangement

Investprop’s platform is offered under the Partner’s regulated brand or an approved co-brand, with the Partner responsible for the regulated wrapper and Investprop responsible for technology and operations.

7. Compliance, Risk & Governance Framework

Investprop has built compliance into the platform as a first-class capability that supports the Partner’s obligations:

  • FICA / KYC: identity verification workflow with document capture and status tracking.
  • POPIA: POPIA-aware data handling with subject-access export for data requests.
  • Audit trail: immutable logging of privileged actions with user, IP, and before/after changes.
  • Investor protection: cooling-off period, risk disclaimers, and an appropriateness questionnaire.
  • SPV governance: Companies Act-aligned structures, investor caps, and legal-document generation.
  • Security: hashed credentials, token rotation, email verification, and role-based access control.
  • Transparency: itemised use-of-funds, financial statements, and downloadable tax certificates.

A joint compliance working group is proposed to align the platform’s controls with the Partner’s policies and the FSCA’s conduct standards.
