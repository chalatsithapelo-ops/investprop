How It Works

From listing to distribution in five clear steps.

STEP 01

Source & vet

Deals enter via the acquisition pipeline or property owners, then get financial and AI risk analysis.

STEP 02

Structure & publish

An SPV is created, a funding goal and use-of-funds breakdown are set, and the campaign goes live.

STEP 03

Invest

FICA-verified investors browse opportunities, model scenarios, and contribute from R10,000.

STEP 04

Own & manage

Share certificates are issued; managers run the flip, rental, or development to completion.

STEP 05

Earn & exit

Income and gains are distributed; investors trade shares on the marketplace to exit.

Every step is tracked, logged, and visible to the right stakeholders — nothing happens in a black box.
